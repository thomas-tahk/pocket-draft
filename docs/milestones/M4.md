# Milestone 4: Draft runs, start to finish

**Done-gate:** "On my phone I open the app, tap Draft, draft and build a deck, then play battles from the run screen until I hit 5 wins or 2 losses. Each battle's result and replay shows on that screen, and it looks and moves like Pocket. I can abandon the draft, leave a battle, or quit the run at any point."

**Status:** passes in a headless browser at phone and desktop size, on both the WebAssembly build the live site uses and the Go server. The one exception is a played 5-win run (below). You haven't tried it yet.

- **Full run on a phone (390×844, touch), WebAssembly build:**
  - A fresh QA agent drafted 20 picks by tap, then went through shop and deckbuild, chose the Energy Zone and finalized.
  - It then played battles from the run screen by real taps and drags on the board, to the end of the run.
  - About 17 battles were played this way across runs. The agent won 3; its play is crude, and the bot beat it.
  - Each result landed on the run screen with a working Replay (step back / forward / play).
  - Runs ended by themselves at 2 losses, on the "Run lost" summary.
- **Not reached by play:** a 5-win run. The agent couldn't win 5. The "Run won" screen was checked by loading a 5–0 run into the browser's storage, and it renders correctly.
- **Leaving, on desktop (1440×900):**
  - Abandon draft mid-way works.
  - Leaving a battle and returning within 90 s resumes the same game. Past 90 s it records a loss.
  - Closing the tab and reopening at 20 s resumes. At 95 s it's a loss.
  - Quit run works.
  - Reloading mid-draft keeps the same offer (no reroll trick).
- **The new draft rules:**
  - No picked card appeared again in 230 recorded packs.
  - The type lean is visible after 1–2 picks.
  - The Energy Zone in battle only ever made the chosen types.
  - Eevee ex was drafted, and evolved into Sylveon in battle.

- **Tests at the head:**
  - Engine and server `go test`, gofmt, lint and build all pass.
  - The browser suite: 131 passed, apart from those 16.
  - A new production-build browser run (`npm run test:e2e:prod`) passes 14/14.

## How to try it

Open the PR's Vercel preview link on your phone.

1. Tap **Draft**. Make 20 picks, use the shop, build 20 cards, choose your **Energy Zone** types, then **Finalize deck**.
2. On the run screen, tap **Next battle** and play. After each battle you're back on the run screen with the result and a **Replay** button.
3. Keep going until 5 wins or 2 losses. The summary appears on its own, and the run is saved under **Past runs**.
4. Try leaving: **Abandon draft** while drafting; in a battle, Menu → **Leave battle**, then come back within 90 s; close the tab mid-battle and reopen it; **Quit run** on the run screen.

Watch for:
- **Draft:**
  - A card you picked never shows up again.
  - After 2–3 Pokémon of one type, that type and its support Trainers show up a lot more.
  - A Trainer you picked makes its Pokémon more likely, and the reverse, e.g. Nemona and Pawmot.
  - Eevee ex can now be drafted, and becomes more likely once you pick an Eeveelution.
- **Deckbuild:** the new **Energy Zone** box sits above your cards. It starts from the energy your attacks need, and you can choose 1–3 types.
- **Look:** a light, Pocket-like style on every screen, with a dark version if your phone is set to dark. Screens fade in briefly, and the fade is off if your phone is set to reduce motion. All screenshots are in [`docs/chief/look/`](../chief/look/).
- **Phone:** a pack fits in two columns. Double-tapping an offer makes only one pick.

**Fixed along the way:**
- **Bot decks lost their evolutions:** once picks stopped repeating, the bot's deckbuilder filled up on Basics. Decks holding an evolution fell from 90 to 29 of 100. It now keeps its pick order ([2779511](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/2779511)).
- **Energy default ignored Dragons:** the picker's first version defaulted from the Pokémon's own types. A Druddigon deck (Fire + Water attacks) could never attack. It now uses the attack costs, the rule you set on 2026-10-18 (merged in [cd976b7](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/cd976b7)).
- **Wrong Marowak:** Kiawe ("Alolan Marowak") was boosting the Fighting Marowak. Raging Bolt was listed as Future. Both fixed ([91e9baa](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/91e9baa)).
- **Late reopen lost the battle:** reopening a closed tab in the last seconds of the 90 could still record a loss, because the timer raced the resume. This only showed on the production build. Fixed, with a new production-build test ([4fe3962](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/4fe3962)).
- **Double-tap double pick:** a quick double-tap on a phone picked two cards from consecutive packs. Fixed ([761f55a](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/761f55a)).
- **Hidden Finalize reason:** "Add a Basic Pokémon to finalize" only showed as a tooltip. It's now visible (same commit).

**Known, not fixed:**
- **Battle board:**
  - The board isn't keyboard-playable: you can't reach cards, Ready or End Turn.
  - Its end-screen buttons are 34–43 px.
  - The "YOUR TURN" banner briefly covers End Turn and your Active's HP.
  - Replay's controls cover your Active's HP.
  - These are board work, planned for later.
- **Back button:** the browser / Android Back leaves the app, because there is one history entry. In a battle that starts the 90 s clock.
- **Reload lands on Home:** reloading on the run screen, the end-of-battle screen or a replay goes to Home with "Continue run", one extra tap. Draft, shop, deckbuild and a left battle resume directly.
- **Link opened during a battle:** a shared-deck link opened while a battle is saved shows the battle and ignores the link.
- **No favicon:** every page load logs a 404 for it.
- **Tap after a drag:** a tap on your Active within ~0.1 s of finishing a drag is ignored.
- **Desktop:** the battle board is a phone-width column in the middle of the page, and the draft leaves most of the page empty.
- **Run screen:** the finished-deck panel (Copy code / Copy link / Export / Start new draft / Home) sits above Next battle on every visit, pushing it ~450 px down on a phone.
- **CI:** CI doesn't run the browser tests at all, so the production-build check runs only when someone runs `npm run test:e2e:prod`.

**Stuck:** none.

## Decisions that need you (most important first)

1. **Merge:** the PR from `m4-draft-runs` into `main` deploys all of M4. Your call.
2. **How the look feels.**
   - Built: a light style, with teal pill buttons, rounded white panels, card frames in the card's type colour, and a rounded system font. A dark version follows your phone's setting.
   - Pocket's brighter cyan was too low-contrast with white text, so the teal is darker.
   - Everything comes from one file (`src/tokens.css`), so a retune is quick. Tell me what feels off.
3. **Type lean strength.**
   - Built: after 3 Water picks, Water Pokémon go from 14% to 49% of offers, and Water-support Trainers become about 4× as common. Other types keep about 27% of offers.
   - There is no cap: at 8 picks of one type the weight is 17×.
   - Softer or stronger once you've played?
4. **Type-support Trainers.** A Trainer counts as support for a type if its text names the type, or it's in a hand-made list:
   - Blaine → Fire; Koga, Piers and Team Galactic Grunt → Darkness; Marlon → Water + Psychic; Lana → Water; Hala → Fighting.
   - Nemona, Clemont, Clemont's Backpack and Sophocles → Lightning; Jasmine → Metal; Iris and Drayden → Dragon.
   - Mallow → Grass + Psychic; Acerola and Budding Expeditioner → Psychic.
   - Squirt Bottle is left out, because it hurts Fire.
   - Trainers spanning several types (Hau, Cynthia, Barry…) are left out.

   Any to change?
5. **Niche groups.**
   - Built: each Fossil goes with its own line only; Eevee Bag with the Eeveelutions; the two Booster Capsules, Professor Sada and Professor Turo with Ancient / Future Pokémon; Lusamine, Beast Wall and Beastite with Ultra Beasts.
   - Open: Koraidon and Miraidon count as Ancient / Future by name, including their older printings. Does Pocket tag those older printings that way?
6. **Eevee ex: one direction or both?**
   - Built: picking an Eeveelution makes Eevee ex 3× as likely.
   - Picking Eevee ex does not make Eeveelutions likelier. Your note ("picking an Eeveelution should raise its odds") reads as one direction. Want both?
7. **Eevee ex rule readings:**
   - It evolves only by playing the Eeveelution from your hand. Deck-evolve effects like Rare Candy still need "Eevee".
   - An effect that removes Abilities (Alolan Muk) also stops it.

   Right?
8. **Energy Zone picker wording.** It reads:

## Learn from it

Escaped bugs get their test and their one-line lesson after you play it. What the gates caught this milestone, and the lessons already written down:
- **Bot side effects:** a draft-rule change silently starved bot decks of evolutions. `TestBotPlaysARealGame` caught it after two branches merged. Lesson: run the bot-play test after every draft-rule merge, not only on the branch.
- **Production-only bugs:** the resume race only showed up on the production build, because dev double-runs effects. The leave/resume tests now also run against a production build (`npm run test:e2e:prod`).
- **Owner answers:** the Energy default contradicted an answer you gave on 2026-10-18. Lesson for builders: grep `docs/decisions/` for the feature's nouns before choosing a default.
