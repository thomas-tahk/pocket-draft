## How to play it

**On your phone (Vercel preview of the PR):**
1. Open the preview link on the PR.
2. Tap Start draft and draft.
3. Go through shop, then deckbuild, then review, and tap **▶ Play deck**.
4. To keep the deck at a full 20, use only the Pokémon you drafted in deckbuild. Poké Ball, Professor's Research and any Trainer can't be played yet; they're left out and the board tells you so.
5. Play the game:
   1. Coin flip.
   2. Drag a Basic to your Active spot, and others to the Bench.
   3. Tap Ready.
   4. On your turn:
      - drag Basics to the Bench
      - drag the Energy Zone onto a Pokémon
      - drag an evolution onto its Pokémon
      - tap your Active and pick an attack, or tap End Turn
6. Hold any card to zoom it. Use the notebook icon (top left) for history, and ☰ to concede.

Quick game without a draft: open `/?battle`. It uses the default decks, which are all Basics.

**Locally:**
```sh
(cd server && go build . && ./server)   # :8080
npm run dev -- --host                   # phone on the same Wi-Fi: http://<mac-ip>:5173
```

## Decisions that need you (most important first)

1. **Decks short of 20.** Deckbuild still adds Poké Ball, Professor's Research, shop staples and under-evolutions. The engine can't play those yet, so they're dropped and you play with fewer than 20. A deck with no Basic shows a raw `400` error, and only a reload gets you out. Should deckbuild change, and how? (Deck rules are yours.) [1.30], [1.31]
2. **Drafted decks that can't attack.** One drafted game ran 43 turns with no attack, because the Energy Zone rolled types the Active couldn't pay for. There is no energy-choice control at deckbuild yet. [1.6], [M1-end]
3. **The bot is weak.** It never benches, evolves or retreats from the default decks, and you won 7 of the 8 test games. How strong it should be for M1 is your call.
4. **Draft pool is 188 cards** (down from 1363): no Trainers, no Abilities, and only 3 ex. It stays that way until card text is built. The start screen still mentions Trainers. [1.30]
5. **Touch feel:**
   - Should tap-tap also work on a phone? Today a plain tap picks a card up. [1.20]
   - Letting go after a long hold drops the card instead of keeping it held. [1.21]
   - Your own opening coin waits for a tap. [1.22]
6. **Ways out:**
   - You can't concede while a choice is waiting.
   - There's no Back or New game mid-game; you have to concede.
   - There's no Retry when the server fails.
   - The old screen had all of these. [1.24], [1.29]
7. **Rule reads:**
   - Hungrily Draw at 10 cards still deals its damage and just draws nothing. Should the attack be unusable instead (T3)?
   - The first player can use a cost-free attack on turn 1, per T5. [M1-end]
8. **Setup:**
   - Can you take a placed card back before Ready?
   - Should the opponent see how many cards you've placed?
   - The bot actually places its cards after your Ready, with its picks hidden. [1.5], [1.22]
9. **Art you'd supply:** points pips (now Poké Balls, but your screenshot shows coins), energy icons, card backs, the coin, and the history icon. Everything is CSS for now. [1.19], [1.22], [1.28]

Everything else is a placeholder for wording, look or timing. Each one is listed with what was done in [`docs/questions/M1.md`](../questions/M1.md), grouped by plan id.
