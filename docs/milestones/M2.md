# Milestone 2: M1 fixes, Trainers, Abilities

**Done-gate:** "On my phone I draft and build a 20-card deck where every card works. Against the bot I play a Supporter, an Item and a Tool, use an Ability, and each one does what its text says."

**Status: passes through the server API; the owner has not yet played it on a phone.**

- The end-of-milestone review built a 20-card deck where every card works and played two games against the bot through the HTTP API, with seeds 1 and 5. Every Trainer and Ability did what its text says:
  - Professor's Research drew 2.
  - Erika healed 20.
  - Poké Ball put a Basic in the hand.
  - Potion took damage from 60 to 40.
  - Giant Cape raised HP from 60 to 80, on both an Active and a Benched Pokémon.
  - Shaymin's Fragrant Flower Garden healed 10 when used.
- The deck was picked by hand because drafting runs in the browser. Deckbuild now offers only working cards, and a deck with a card the engine can't play is refused with a message.
- The review found 10 bugs, and all of them are fixed. The milestone head passes:
  - 133 engine tests and 369 server tests
  - `gofmt`, lint and build
  - 40 Playwright tests at 390×844
- `public/pd.wasm` is rebuilt at the head.
- Each Trainer and Ability wording has one Go test on a real card. Nobody has checked them by eye in a real game.

## How to play it

**On your phone (Vercel preview of the PR):**
1. Open the preview link on the PR, tap Start draft and draft.
2. In deckbuild, every card offered now works. Build 20 with at least one Basic, then tap **▶ Play deck**.
3. The coin flips by itself, then shows who goes first.
4. Setup:
   - Drag or tap-tap Basics to your Active and Bench.
   - Before you tap Ready, you can drag a placed card back to your hand or move it, and moving onto a filled spot swaps the two cards.
   - The bot's placements show face down.
   - Tap **Ready**.
5. On your turn:
   - **Supporter or Item:** drag it onto the board. If it needs a target, drop it on the lit Pokémon.
   - **Tool:** drag it onto one of your Pokémon. Its name shows under the Pokémon.
   - **Stadium:** drag it onto the Stadium spot.
   - **Ability:** tap your Active and use the Ability row on its card, or tap a Benched Pokémon that pulses. If there are several targets they light up, with Cancel.
   - Energy, evolve, attack and End Turn work as in M1.
6. ☰ → Concede ends the game at once, even while a choice is waiting.

Quick game without a draft: open `/?battle`. It uses the default decks.

**Locally:**
```sh
(cd server && go build . && ./server)   # :8080
npm run dev -- --host                   # phone on the same Wi-Fi: http://<mac-ip>:5173
```

## What was built (28 issues, branch `m2-trainers-abilities`)

**M1 fixes:**
- Every deck is 20 working cards with a Basic, for you and for the bot.
- Concede works from any state.
- The opening coin flips by itself.
- Setup lets you take back and move cards, and the bot places before your Ready, face down.
- Tap-tap is confirmed on touch.

**Rules:**
- Special Conditions, and clearing them on retreat or evolve.
- The Checkup in rule order.
- Coin flips as events.
- Rule hooks that cards can change.

**Trainers:**
- Loading Trainers and playing them from the screen.
- Tools, Stadiums (including "use the Stadium") and Fossils.
- Deck search.
- All Supporter batches, Items, and the Tool/Stadium batch.

**Abilities:**
- Use an Ability, from your Active's card or from the Bench.
- Always-on Abilities, Abilities that react to events, "when you evolve" Abilities, and Abilities that change a rule.
- Three batches of Ability texts.

**Draft pool:** 431 cards, up from 188 at the end of M1:
- 157 Trainers: 77 Supporters, 41 Items, about 26 Tools and about 13 Stadiums
- about 98 Pokémon with an Ability

**Test infrastructure:** `POST /api/new` takes an optional `bot` deck. The e2e tests that depend on the board pin both decks, so new card texts no longer break them.

**Fixed at milestone end (review):**
- Abilities couldn't be aimed at the opponent's Pokémon.
- Protective Poncho was ignored.
- Giant Cape's HP was ignored for Benched knockouts.
- Alolan Muk didn't stop "when damaged" Abilities.
- Claydol didn't stop Leftovers, Sitrus Berry, Soothing Shore and some Supporters.
- 12 evolutions whose earlier stage doesn't work could be drafted and could never be played.
- A knocked-out Benched Pokémon fired the Active's Tool.
- A latent nil crash.
- gofmt failures.

## Decisions that need you (most important first)

1. **Choices the game makes for you.** Several cards should let you pick, but there's no picker yet, so the game picks:

   | Card | What the game picks |
   |---|---|
   | Electrical Cord | the leftmost Benched Pokémon |
   | Dawn | the Energy type, in a fixed order |
   | Silver | the first Supporter in the opponent's hand |
   | Team Rocket's Boss | benches every Basic that fits |
   | Professor Sada | all 3 Energy go to one Pokémon |

   Should each get a real picker in M3? These need a picker and stay unplayable until there is one: Hiker, Morty, May.
2. **"When you evolve" Abilities** (Ignition, Dig Up, Unruly Claw…) are a button you can use on the turn the Pokémon evolved. Pocket prompts you as you evolve. Should it prompt instead?
3. **What a blocker blocks:**
   - Heal Block (Claydol) stops every heal, including Supporters and Items.
   - Flower Shield (Comfey) blocks Special Conditions only.
   - Clear Veil undoes an attack's effects on that Pokémon, but the damage still lands.

   Are these right?
4. **Named cards match the exact name.** Lt. Surge works with Raichu, not Raichu ex or Alolan Raichu. Koga works with Muk, not Alolan Muk. Barry, Blaine and Cynthia work the same way. Does Pocket count the ex and regional forms?
5. **Cards that would do nothing can't be played.** For example, Potion with no damage, Guzma with no Tools, or Piers with no Energy on the opponent's Active. Poké Ball with no Basic left is still playable and finds nothing. Is that right?
6. **Ten in hand:** a draw Trainer can't be played at 10 cards, and the Trainer counts toward the 10. Is that right?
7. **Reveals** (Hand Scope, Red Card, Looker, Silver) show only in the play-by-play text, with no reveal screen. Is that OK for now?
8. **Labels the card data doesn't have.** Ancient, Future and Ultra Beast are fixed lists of names. Koraidon and Miraidon from B2a are left out of Ancient/Future. Memory Light uses the earliest printing of each earlier stage. Beastite counts up to 3 points.
9. **About 60 card texts are skipped** because they need engine work: knock-out or coin triggers, type changes, two Tools, Energy that counts as 2, attack locks, and looking at the opponent's hand. Their cards stay out of the pool. The full list is in [`docs/questions/M2.md`](../questions/M2.md), under 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6.
10. **The pool is still 431 of 1363 cards.** 845 are out only because an attack text isn't built, which is M3 scope. Another 32 have a working Ability but an unmapped attack: Heatran, Raichu, Arceus ex, Giratina ex, Solgaleo ex and others.
11. **Checkup coins:** your Burn and Sleep flips wait for your tap, even at the end of the bot's turn. The Checkup checks P1's Active, then P2's. Pocket may flip these by itself.
12. **A Fossil in play** has no button to discard it. The engine move exists. Where should the button go?
13. **Wording and look placeholders:**
    - Trainer drag hints
    - the Tool name label under a Pokémon
    - the yellow no-target frame
    - "Took back" and "Moved" in the history
    - the deckbuild intro still says the free universals are "always available"
    - Finalize's tooltip
14. **Order to merge:** this PR is based on M1 ([#91](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/pull/91)). Merge #91 first.

Every other interpretation is listed with what was done in [`docs/questions/M2.md`](../questions/M2.md), grouped by plan id.
