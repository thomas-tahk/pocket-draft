**Done-gate:** "I play a practice game against the bot at `/?battle`: every energy shows as a symbol, every card drawn or played visibly moves, damage visibly lands, and the bot attacks, evolves and can win."

**Status: passes in a headless browser with animations on; the owner has not played it yet.**

- The review played 2 full games against the bot through the screen, at 390×844 and at 1280×800, with the bot drafting its own deck.
  - Seed 5: you won 3–2 on turn 18. The bot attacked 8 times, evolved twice and played 2 Trainers.
  - Seed 21: the bot won 3–1 on turn 13. It attacked 5 times, evolved once and played 4 Trainers.
- No energy shows a letter anywhere. 134–173 animations ran per game.
- After every animation, after Skip mid-animation and after Concede mid-playback, every card was back at rest. No console errors.
- Tests at the head: engine and server `go test`, `gofmt`, lint and build pass, plus 53 Playwright tests. `public/pd.wasm` is rebuilt.

## How to play it

Open the PR's Vercel preview on desktop, then go to `/?battle`, or draft and tap **▶ Play deck**. On a phone the battle screen works, but the draft screens are still the old desktop layout (that is M4).

Watch for:
- Energy icons on the Energy Zone, attack costs, attached Energy, retreat cost, Ability text, the history and the type counts in draft and deckbuild.
- Cards sliding as they're drawn, played, benched, evolved, retreated or knocked out. A Trainer passes through the middle of the board on its way to the discard.
- Energy flying from the Zone, the attacker leaning in, a red or green number, the HP bar draining.
- The bot benching, evolving, playing Trainers and Abilities, and attacking.

Animations are off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Glyph sheet: [`m3-energy-icons.png`](m3-energy-icons.png).

**Fixed along the way:**
- The server crashed on state reads once a Stadium could be used ([65754a2](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/65754a2)).
- With two copies of one card, the wrong card animated ([76d706d](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/76d706d)).
- The opponent's played card ballooned to about 2.4× and covered the board ([0159ac7](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/pocket-draft/commit/0159ac7)).

**Known, not fixed:**
- If both copies of a card are in play, they can still swap which one animates. A real fix needs a per-card id from the engine.
- After Skip, your own start-of-turn draw doesn't animate.
- The screen has no button for using a Stadium. The server accepts the move, and this gap predates M3.
