Plan for approval · 25 Oct 2026 · M5

Every card works

Builds after M4, as its own branch and one pull request into main. One approval covers all of it.

Done when

On my phone I draft from every card in the game, and every card I play does what its text says. Coin flips show in a grid, conditions show as icons on the Pokémon, choices show the cards and pick themselves when there's only one, and an attack shows its damage before I confirm.

Today 1,672 of 3,879 printings work, so the draft only offers 433 cards.

What gets built, in build order

  1. A Basic played on your side that you didn't play

    The bug from your play-test. Reproduce it first, then fix it, so the bot can never act for you.

    Check: a test that plays turns against the bot and fails if any Pokémon reaches your side without your move. #123

  2. Choices show the cards

    Every in-battle choice shows the actual cards, can always be finished, and picks itself when only one option exists.

    Check: a browser test with a one-option choice (no prompt) and a multi-option one (card images, completes). #122

  3. Coin flips and condition icons on the board

    Flips show in a grid 3 wide: you tap the first coin, the rest flip on their own. Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, Paralyzed and Confused show as icons on the Pokémon.

    Check: browser tests for a 3-flip attack and each condition. #56 #57

    Independent of 1–2

  4. Coin-flip and condition attack texts

    Flip for bonus damage, per heads, until tails, flip to fail, and attacks that inflict conditions.

    Check: one test per wording, on a real card. #58 #59 #60

    After 3

  5. The rest of the attack texts

    Energy, damage changes, switching and Bench damage, draw / search / heal, can't-attack and can't-retreat locks, and the leftovers, in 8 batches.

    Check: one test per wording, on a real card. #80–#87

    Alongside 4

  6. Damage you can see before you commit

    Orange badges show when a damage modifier is active, and tapping one lists each source. The enlarged card shows the final damage (after Weakness and modifiers) before you confirm an attack.

    Check: a browser test where a Stadium boosts an attack: the badge shows and the preview number matches the damage dealt. #68 #111

    After 5

  7. Every card in the draft

    A coverage test proves every card text has a working effect. Then the "working cards only" draft filter is deleted, and the draft offers every card.

    Check: the coverage test passes over all 3,879 printings, and the draft pool equals the full card list. #88

    Last

Calls I made, so you can overrule them

  • Cards before real opponents. The M4 plan listed real opponents next. I swapped them: a match against a real player with half the cards missing is a thin game, and real opponents need your approval for the hosting cost (about $3.69 a month). Real opponents become M6.
  • Card rules I'm unsure of: built as my best guess from the card text and your earlier answers, and each one listed in the M5 report for you to check.
  • Cheaper model for the text batches. The repetitive card-text batches run on the cheapest model; any batch that fails its tests is redone on the normal one.
